01 · Context

The problem & my role.

Pharmaceutical vendor documents arrive in scanned and digital formats. This ongoing externship focuses on extracting and classifying their contents, then retrieving useful information through a Python document-intelligence pipeline.

AI Document Intelligence Extern

My work covers OCR implementation and evaluation, retrieval experiments, and technical documentation. The project is part of the Extern Pfizer Program, with a focus on how different tools and pipeline choices affect output quality.

02 · Process

How the work came together.

  1. 01

    Understand how the models read

    Produced a written explanation of how large language models tokenize text, learn token-level patterns during training, and predict the next token during inference. This research informed the approach to an extraction-focused pipeline.

  2. 02

    Extract and classify document content

    Implementing and evaluating Tesseract and PaddleOCR workflows for scanned and digital pharmaceutical documents. The work aims to turn varied document layouts into structured information suitable for downstream classification and retrieval.

  3. 03

    Build the retrieval workflow

    Developing retrieval-augmented generation with LlamaIndex and vector search. Experiments with text chunking and large language models focus on how document content is organized and retrieved.

  4. 04

    Evaluate the complete pipeline

    Evaluating OCR accuracy, retrieval quality, and routing performance, while documenting model trade-offs and deployment recommendations. These evaluations are ongoing; the project is still under development.

The current work evaluates how well the system reads documents, retrieves relevant content, and routes information. The goal is to document a defensible technical approach and deployment recommendations as the prototype develops.
