01 · Context

The problem & my role.

Sales, marketing, product, and checkout data were spread across disconnected files. I built an ETL workflow and a queryable SQLite database to support marketing performance, inventory analysis, and planning for the holiday season.

SQL & Database Architecture Extern

I worked across source inventory, data cleaning, relational modeling, SQL analysis, and visualization. SQL was reviewed and debugged with Claude, and the analysis was translated into an interactive dashboard and a Holiday Game Plan presented to Breaking Games.

02 · Process

How the work came together.

  1. 01

    Inventory the raw data

    Cataloged six sources and used SQLite queries to answer initial business questions: leading products by units and revenue, product counts, advertising spend, and unique checkouts. Recorded the queries and findings in a Week 1 playbook.

  2. 02

    Create a reliable relational foundation

    Standardized product identifiers and created a dim_product dimension table. INNER and LEFT JOIN queries connected scattered sales and marketing tables, supporting a product-performance summary and a view of customer journeys.

  3. 03

    Connect marketing spend to sales

    Cleaned the channel taxonomy and analyzed attribution across more than 400 referral sources. Campaign-level analysis connected Meta spending with sales to support recommendations about Q4 marketing priorities.

  4. 04

    Investigate checkout noise

    Examined shipping ratios, quantity outliers, and repeat non-converters. SQL filtering isolated bot patterns and invalid records, and abandonment metrics were recalculated using cleaner data and aligned comparison periods.

  5. 05

    Turn the analysis into a decision tool

    Built an interactive HTML and Chart.js dashboard covering product performance, traffic channels, and cart analysis. Presented the dashboard and Holiday Game Plan with inventory reorder and marketing-priority recommendations.

03 · The work

Deliverables & tools.

Validated analytics database

A SQLite database integrating six sources with consistent product identifiers, a shared product dimension, and reusable joined queries.

Interactive holiday dashboard

Holiday Game Plan

A documented set of findings and recommendations for inventory reorders, marketing allocation, and checkout investigation.
